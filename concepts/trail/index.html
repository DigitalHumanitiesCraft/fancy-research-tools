About this investigation

A catalogue investigation of musical instruments in the Metropolitan Museum of Art. Compare material descriptions, original date wording, and attribution qualifiers before recording an interpretation.

The snapshot contains the 60 lowest numeric IDs returned for the Musical Instruments department on 10 October 2026. It is a convenience sample, not representative of the collection or musical history. Missing values mean missing catalogue information.

Met Open Access source. Downloads include the source snapshot manifest and the unmodified selected records.
