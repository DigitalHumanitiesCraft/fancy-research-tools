Visiting the page

This page sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and loads no content from third parties. Fonts and images are served from the same server as the page.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and reliable delivery under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Details, including the transfer to the United States, are given in the GitHub privacy statement.
