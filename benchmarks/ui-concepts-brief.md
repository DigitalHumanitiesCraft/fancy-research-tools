# Three interaction concepts

Provisional research episode: investigate how material and attribution are described in the frozen Met instrument sample. Find records mentioning wood, select two to four for close comparison, inspect original dates and attribution qualifiers, write an observation and an unresolved question, and download the selected source records with the note and source provenance. This is catalogue analysis, not a claim about historical prevalence. Data: data/met-instruments/records.json and manifest.json, unchanged across concepts. No synthetic records or invented images.

All concepts support search, inspect, add/remove a comparison selection, observation + question, and real JSON export including source IDs/URLs and manifest. Source values remain unchanged. Notes are user interpretation, separately identified. Empty search and empty export selection have usable states. No runtime CDN or external AI. English UI. Keyboard, 320px narrow view, system fonts, own design identity. The whole collection need not be forced into one viewport.

A: object-led catalogue, editorial discovery, then collection tray and comparison.
B: comparison-first workbench, purposeful table/matrix and selection, large evidence comparison surface.
C: question-led investigation, coherent steps with revisitable state from finding material through comparing evidence to recording observation.

Alternatives share the task, not a mandatory layout. The earlier catalogue remains at `/baseline/` for lookup and filtering. The current `/pilot/` investigation is an additional candidate, with its own source identity and technical evidence. All four investigations support validated export/reopen and local recovery. Researcher validation has not occurred.
