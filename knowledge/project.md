"fancy (research) tools!" is the offer of Digital Humanities Craft OG for agentic engineering, the development of tools, workflows and knowledge bases with frontier language models and coding agents. It addresses research and cultural heritage institutions as well as companies and public administration, and offers the work as training, together with the client's team or on commission. The provider's own research tools and methods serve as evidence of what the work produces and can be adapted.

## Location

The page is a subpage of dhcraft.org, at https://dhcraft.org/fancy-research-tools/ in German and https://dhcraft.org/en/fancy-research-tools/ in English, with privacy statements at `/fancy-research-tools/datenschutz/` and `/en/fancy-research-tools/privacy/`. ## Context

The name comes from a remark in a chat about a colleague's prompted markup editor for an edition project. That editor showed the pattern the page offers, a tool shaped around the person doing most of the work. The page began on 2026-10-01 as a standalone service page for custom and adaptable research tools. On 2026-10-02 it became the offer for agentic engineering ([ADR-013](specification.md#adr-013-positioning-as-an-agentic-engineering-offer)) and moved into the dhcraft.org site ([ADR-014](specification.md#adr-014-the-page-moves-into-the-dhcraftorg-site)). The [journal](journal.md) records the steps.

## Purpose

Visitors are archives, museums, libraries, universities, research projects, companies and administrations with a recurring workflow and no in-house development, and teams that want to learn to work with coding agents themselves. They should understand the offer, judge whether it fits and send a request, either as a conversation or as documents from which a first prototype is built. Billing follows the task, per hour, per working day or as a flat fee, starting from the client's budget.

## Technical realisation

The dhcraft.org site is an Astro project that builds static pages and is deployed by GitHub Actions on every push to its main branch. The page takes its copy and structured data from `src/i18n/fancy.ts` and is rendered by `src/components/fancy/` inside the site's layout, navigation and footer. Its content needs no script and loads nothing from third parties. This repository supplies knowledge, image prompts and the scripts for images and checks, and its own GitHub Pages site is switched off. Storage zones of both repositories are listed in [INDEX](INDEX.md#storage-zones).

## Scope

The page offers and explains. The tools run at their own addresses. The page holds no form, account or payment flow, and requests go by e-mail or telephone.
