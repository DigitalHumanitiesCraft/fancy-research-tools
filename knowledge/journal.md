# Journal

## 2026-10-01

The name comes from a chat about a colleague's prompted markup editor for her edition project, a tool shaped around the person doing most of the work, with corpus-wide search, consistency checks, issues at the document and a preview of the target platform.

The positioning went through three stages in one session. A showcase of the own research tools was replaced by a volunteer collective, which was then replaced by a professional, research-grounded service of Digital Humanities Craft OG. The final framing widens the audience to companies and public administration under the name "fancy (research) tools!" and offers the research tools for adaptation, each with its own request button.

Published as the public repository DigitalHumanitiesCraft/fancy-research-tools with GitHub Pages.

A second round the same evening reworked copy and identity. The copy was rewritten against the house writing rules. Three methods and frameworks (Promptotyping, Grounded Vault, Agentic Edition Pipeline) received hand-drawn diagrams, three further tools came from a sweep of the public repositories, and each state line links its evidence. The identity moved to a white ground with a prism mark, a spectrum at matched lightness, EB Garamond, Instrument Sans and JetBrains Mono. The request section offers a conversation or documents for a first prototype and names the billing modes.

Kept off the page after the sweep: the date-extraction timeline (no licence file, header names an internal university training), vetmed-berichtswesen (no Pages site), M³GIM (open rights question in the vault).
