## Open decisions and dependencies

- Real cases with their effort, as evidence that a small budget suffices, need the consent of the projects behind them.
- Offers for work on client data name the model access and who acts as provider of an AI system under the EU AI Act, a matter for the offer template and a legal review, not for the page.
- The conditions under which client research data enter GAMS are not documented. The dhcraft.org home page says archiving is included, the offers in the operator's vault bill it separately. The page says only that archiving is offered through the framework agreement.
- coOCR/HTR keeps no review state per line or page, so the quality claim on expert control holds for it only in part.
- coOCR/HTR puts its code under CC BY 4.0 against the house rule of MIT for code. An external contributor's code came in under CC BY 4.0, so a change needs that contributor's consent or a legal check.
